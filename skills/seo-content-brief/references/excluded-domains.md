# Excluded competitor sources

Filter sources that rank but are not comparable business competitors.

## Common exclusions

- Encyclopedias and broad reference sites
- Social media, video, Q&A, and community platforms
- Generic publishing and blogging platforms
- Search engines
- Marketplaces and e-commerce aggregators
- Forums
- General news and media outlets
- Data/research platforms that are not competing businesses
- Directories, reviews, maps, and listing services
- Comparison aggregators
- Job boards
- SEO and marketing-tool educational pages
- AI assistant platforms
- Government and academic domains

Representative domains include Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Medium,
Amazon, eBay, Stack Overflow, BBC, Forbes, Statista, Yelp, Trustpilot, Semrush,
Ahrefs, Moz, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Apply the rule by source type, not only
by a static list.

## Excluded URL patterns

Exclude tag, author, category/archive, feed, API/admin, login/signup, cart/checkout,
legal-policy, sitemap, and robots URLs.

If fewer than three genuine competitors remain, state that the competitive
landscape is thin and do not fill the table with non-comparable domains.
